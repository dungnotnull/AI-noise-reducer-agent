
# CLAUDE.md

## AI Noise Reducer Agent (Anti AI Slop)

### Vision
Build the world's most trusted signal-extraction engine that transforms noisy, AI-generated, SEO-inflated content into verified, evidence-backed knowledge.

## Product North Star
Convert 10 minutes of reading into 60 seconds of verified understanding.

## Core Capabilities
- AI Slop Detection
- Information Density Scoring
- Evidence Extraction
- Fact Compression
- Source Trust Scoring
- Knowledge Accumulation
- Continuous Research Learning

## Architecture Principles
1. Retrieval before generation.
2. Evidence before summary.
3. Classification before LLM.
4. Human-verifiable outputs.
5. Continuous benchmark evaluation.
6. Prefer existing SOTA open models before training.

## Preferred Stack
### NLP
- ModernBERT
- DeBERTa-v3
- BGE Embeddings
- Jina Embeddings
- E5

### LLM Layer
- GPT-4o-mini
- Gemini Flash
- Qwen
- Llama

### Knowledge
- Qdrant
- Neo4j
- PostgreSQL

## Golden Rule
Every generated insight must be traceable back to source evidence.
